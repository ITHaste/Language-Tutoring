About Polyglot Hub

Our mission is to make language learning accessible, personal, and effective.

Founded by Passionate Language Learners

Polyglot Hub was started by Kevin van Dijken and Calvina Risky Amelia, two language enthusiasts who believe in the power of human connection. Frustrated with rigid and impersonal learning apps, they set out to create a platform that focuses on real conversation with native speakers.

We believe that the best way to learn a language is to speak it. Our tutors are not just teachers; they are your partners in a journey to fluency. They tailor each lesson to your personal goals, whether you're learning for travel, work, or personal enrichment.

Join us and start your language adventure today!

Frequently Asked Questions
